      Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough

      PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 at roughly 200 hours, all offline, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals and no mission replay. The plan below follows its three steps: finish the story and do the one missable thing first, then the 100% checklist, then Strangers & Freaks and cleanup. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots) and keep cheats off — they switch trophies and saving off.

      Missables & watch — what can actually go wrong

      Only one trophy is possibly missable. The rest of this page is the watch list: side lines that are character-locked or that go quiet for a chapter, and the three trophies whose method is still unconfirmed or disputed. Everything else can be done after the story in free-roam.
